### Methodology: the T-BAR model
1. **Alter Thoughts**
2. **Modify Behaviour**
3. **Change Action**
4. **Enhance Results**

Philosophy line: first shift people's *will* to act, then build the *skill* to do it well.

**Four-step experiential learning process** (inward to outward):
1. Learning by Observation
2. Learning by Reflection
3. Learning by Active Experimentation (case studies, live simulations, activities, exercises)
4. Installation of New Learning

## 3. Services (6 service lines)

1. **Experiential Training**: instructor-led workshops (in-person, virtual live, outbound) for junior staff up to CXO. Covers behaviour transformation, communication excellence and workplace competency.
2. **Leadership Journeys**: learning spread over time to build skill mastery, with role-based learning paths and business impact evaluation.
3. **Leadership Coaching**: "here and now" performance coaching that broadens perspective. Individual or group.
4. **Assessment & Development Centre**: assesses skills, role fit and development needs. Partners with L&D on high-potential identification.
5. **360 Feedback Survey**: designs the rater matrix and custom dimensions, writes business-relevant situational judgment items, and runs a fully anonymous survey.
6. **On-Demand Leadership Development Consultant**: annual retainer for startups and SMEs. Covers competency baselining, needs analysis, program design, workshops, certification programs and coaching.

## 13. Revamp direction

### Proposed sitemap (6 sections)
```
Home
├── Programs        (Leadership · Managerial · Communication · Outdoor: Sports & Horses · Book-based)
│     └── /programs/<slug>  one page per course, filterable by level / format / duration
├── Services        (Experiential Training · Leadership Journeys · Coaching · Assessment & 360 · L&D Retainer)
├── Certification   (Master Trainer: modules, outcomes, batch dates, fees, enquiry form)
├── About           (Approach / T-BAR · Team · Founder & Keynotes · Clients)
├── Impact          (Case studies · Named testimonials · Gallery · Insights/blog)
└── Contact         (Enquiry form + WhatsApp · Map · Phone & hours · Policies)
```

### Homepage sections, top to bottom
1. **Hero**: one-line promise (e.g. "Leadership workshops that change how your managers think and lead.") + "Get a customized workshop" CTA + WhatsApp button
2. **Stat strip**: 23,000+ lives · 12,500+ hours · 23+ years · 90+ client brands
3. **Client logo marquee**
4. **What we do**: 6 service cards
5. **How we work**: T-BAR model visual (Thoughts → Behaviour → Action → Results)
6. **Popular programs**: 6 best-seller cards showing duration and format
7. **Certification banner**: Master Trainer, next batch, enquire
8. **Founder block**: Harshita's photo, credentials, keynote CTA
9. **Testimonials**: name, role, company
10. **Enquiry form + map + footer**

### Design system
- **Palette (brown, warm, minimal):** espresso `#3B2A20` (text, footer) · walnut `#6B4A35` (headings, secondary) · caramel `#B5793F` (single CTA accent) · sand `#E9DCCB` (cards, borders) · cream `#FAF6F0` (page background) · white for contrast sections
- **Type:** one serif for headings (e.g. Fraunces / Playfair Display) + clean sans for body (e.g. Inter / DM Sans)
- **Look:** generous whitespace, real workshop photos (not stock icons), rounded corners, subtle borders and no heavy shadows or gradients, one accent colour only
- **Tone:** plain, confident and specific. Say what they do and for whom. No buzzword fluff.
- Mobile-first and fast. Every page has one primary CTA: **"Get a customized workshop."**

### SEO basics
- Unique title and meta description per page, written from real page content
- Clean slugs plus 301 redirects from every old URL above
- LocalBusiness schema (Bengaluru, Indiranagar), Course schema on program pages
- Embedded map on Contact
